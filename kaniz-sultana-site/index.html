Meet Kaniz Sultana

Two decades of care, skill and confidence

Kaniz Sultana founded Exclusive Kaniz Hair & Beauty Parlor on 19 October 2005. It is a premium, women-only parlor in Sonadanga, Khulna, offering international-standard beauty care in a private and comfortable space.

The parlor is fully women-powered. Kaniz employs and trains a team of more than 20 women. Many clients have been coming to her for over 15 years, and she still supervises the floor herself.

“Over the past two decades, we have remained committed to fostering female empowerment while delivering an exceptional, luxurious experience for our clientele.”Kaniz Sultana, CEO & Founder
